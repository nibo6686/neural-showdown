> **Why this slice matters.** All 38 adopted rows are own-perspective. Every
> later public property needs one answer to "which opponent Pokémon is this
> protocol line about, and what do we publicly know about who it is?" (for
> example HP, status, boosts, items, abilities, Tera, effects and move
> history). Today that answer is spread across the public readers and the
> v2 extractor. This slice makes it **one provider**, exposed through K, with
> provenance.
>
> ### Part A — public association provider
> 1. Add `sim-core/src/public_association.ts`, a pure function of the
>    **public protocol prefix** (plus the owner's own addressed requests for
>    own-side identity). It builds, per side, the public roster:
>    - **Stints:** each public appearance (switch, drag, replace, Revival
>      Blessing return), with the boundary where it started and ended.
>    - **Appearance vs identity.** The displayed species/details are what the
>      protocol shows. The **identity** is known only when publicly proven:
>      a revealed Illusion (`replace`) re-binds that stint to the true
>      Pokémon **from the reveal onward** and marks the earlier part of the
>      stint as "appeared as X, identity proven later". **Never relabel
>      earlier boundaries' knowledge.** That would be future information at
>      those cursors.
>    - **Roster:** the slots seen so far, the team size from `|teamsize|`,
>      and unseen members as explicit unknown placeholders (never invented
>      IDs).
>
>    Follow the Prompt58 rows for K01 `roster_species`/`active_slot`/
>    `roster_size` current and K17 `appearance`/`identity_reveal`
>    (`docs/planning/perspective-fidelity-prompt58-reconciliation.md`, rows
>    31–34 and 73).
> 2. **Reuse, don't fork.** Build on the B3 direct-switch binding, W3.1/W3.2
>    switch authority (pivots, drags, Red Card, Eject Button/Pack, Emergency
>    Exit, Shed Tail) and the existing Illusion/replace subset. Where the v2
>    extractor already computes the same association, make it **call the new
>    provider** instead of keeping a second copy. The 0-mismatch public
>    differential must stay at 0.
> 3. **Own side.** Own current `roster_species`, `party_position`,
>    `active_slot` and `roster_size` come from the owner's addressed current
>    request, bound to the held original slots (no names as identity). Own
>    appearance under one's own Illusion is the public appearance, while own
>    identity is known.

> ### Part B — K adoption (11 rows) and the public-row proof
> 1. **Rows:**
>    - K01 current `roster_species`, `active_slot`, `roster_size` (own and
>      opponent);
>    - K01 current `party_position` (own);
>    - K17 `appearance`, `identity_reveal` (own and opponent).
>
>    Publish them through the K projection under the existing host/lease
>    authority, with provenance (the protocol line index for public facts,
>    the request for own facts). Unseen opponent members are explicit
>    unknown. Opponent identity under a possible Illusion stays
>    **hypothesis-free**: K reports the appearance and "identity not proven".
>    It doesn't guess.
> 2. **K differential for opponent rows** (`tools/replay_repro/knowledge_diff.cjs`):
>    - **soundness:** every known value equals simulator truth for that
>      slot at that boundary;
>    - **parity:** where the v2 public view has the same fact, the two must
>      agree;
>    - "unknown" is counted, not treated as a mismatch.
> 3. **Causality and leakage.** Extend the W9L leakage-invariance cases
>    (`sim-core/tests/helpers/decision_frame_cases.ts`) to the **K projection**:
>    - two born sessions that differ only in an unrevealed opponent bench
>      member's species, and in whether a Zoroark's Illusion disguises it;
>    - the viewer's K must be byte-identical until the public reveal, and
>      differ only after it, for both owners.
> 4. **Born proofs** (each for both owners):
>    - an Illusion stint before and after `replace`; earlier boundaries
>      unchanged after the reveal;
>    - Illusion broken by fainting;
>    - a drag (Roar/Dragon Tail) and a Red Card;
>    - a pivot;
>    - Revival Blessing returning a fainted member;
>    - an Imposter/Transform appearance, which must **not** change identity;
>    - team size with unseen members;
>    - a forced switch after a faint.
>
>    Each proof has a negative control: foreign, stale or copied handles
>    reject, and default output stays byte-identical.
> 5. **Red tests owned:** `illusion` (24), `forced_switch` (3) and the
>    `pipeline_episode` unbound-switch-source tests (4). Migrate each to born
>    sessions or simulation-only, or retire it with a reason. Then promote
>    the proven rows with exact `limits`. Anything unproven stays `missing`
>    with its reason.
> 6. **Curated fixtures.** The 29 real-replay fixtures in `test:fast` now
>    also check the new rows: 0 K mismatches. Assert completeness at a named
>    reveal point: the Illusion fixture's identity is known from the
>    `replace` line onward.
