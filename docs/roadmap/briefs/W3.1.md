> ### Part A — owner-chosen mid-turn entries (pivots)
> U-turn, Flip Turn, Volt Switch, Parting Shot, Teleport, Shed Tail, Chilly
> Reception and Baton Pass. In each case **the owner chooses** the
> replacement through a mid-turn switch request, so the authority source is
> the same as for a direct switch: the owner's submitted, delivered choice for
> that request, recorded in the request delivery ledger. Extend the existing
> B3 binding (the direct-switch occurrence seam in `owner_identity_events.ts`
> and `owner_identity_crosswalk.ts`) so the entering actor binds to that
> choice occurrence. **Don't** bind by name, species, slot ordinal or
> matching values. Ownership flows only from the real request → choice →
> native switch writer chain, exactly as B3 does.
> - Shed Tail also transfers a Substitute. Baton Pass transfers boosts and
>   volatiles. The binding covers identity only; whatever transfer semantics
>   are already typed stay as they are, and any that aren't are noted, not
>   invented.
> - Chilly Reception's weather and Teleport's priority quirks don't affect
>   binding. Just make sure the request/choice path is the same.
>
> ### Part B — engine-chosen entries (measure, then implement what appears)
> Drags (Roar, Whirlwind, Dragon Tail, Circle Throw), Red Card, Eject
> Button/Eject Pack (owner-chosen via request) and Emergency Exit/Wimp Out
> (owner-chosen) didn't show up as *first* blockers. They may appear once
> pivots pass. For owner-chosen ones, use the Part A mechanism. **Drags pick
> the entering Pokémon inside the engine.** Their authority source is the
> engine's own switch writer occurrence during that action, not an owner
> choice. Bind through the native writer hook (as B2/B3 observe native
> writers), never by inference from the public log. Implement whichever of
> these the full corpus run shows. If drag binding needs a new native hook
> that widens B2 authority, stop and describe it instead of building it.
>
