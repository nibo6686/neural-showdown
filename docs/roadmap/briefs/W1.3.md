> ### Part A — K08 flags and lock (owned, request and current)
> Ten registry rows, all `missing`/high: `disabled`, `maybe_disabled`,
> `trapped`, `maybe_trapped` and `lock`, in both the `request` and
> `current` contexts, own perspective. Follow the approved semantics in
> `docs/planning/perspective-fidelity-prompt61-design.md` ("Emitted flags
> and the real scope mismatch") and the Prompt58 K08 rows:
> - **Capture source.** Literal presence and values come from the same
>   canonical `getMoveRequestData`/`getMoves` return and the selected
>   receipt that W1.2's move bridge uses. Extend `native-owner-moves/v1`, or
>   add `.../flags`, under the same host/lease authority. **Never publish
>   native volatiles or `trapped` state directly.**
> - **Per-option `disabled`** is request-declared: exact Boolean when
>   emitted, **omitted means unknown**. `false` under restricted data is not
>   proof that nothing hidden disables the move.
> - **`trapped` / `maybeTrapped`** are actor facts when emitted **true**.
>   Omission is unknown, never false: the writer can suppress both.
> - **`maybeDisabled` / `maybeLocked`** are **actor-level aggregates**. Emit
>   them under `emitted_flags` with the actor as subject, presence, literal
>   `true` and `affected_options: unspecified`. **Don't** copy them onto
>   every move. The per-move K08 `maybe_disabled` cells stay **blocked**
>   (`unsupported_writer`) until an attribution rule is approved; say so in
>   the registry `limits`.
> - **`lock`.** `maybeLocked` is uncertainty, not the K08 definite `lock`
>   fact. Adopt `lock` (request context) only as the exact **request
>   restriction**: which options the request permits, from the emitted
>   data. A lock **reason** (choice item, Encore, Outrage/locked move,
>   Recharge, Struggle) is public knowledge only when separately proven by
>   public evidence already typed, such as an announced Encore start or a
>   revealed Choice item plus a move use. Otherwise the reason stays
>   unknown. **No reason may be inferred from a single permitted option,
>   a trapped flag or a private locked-move getter.**
> - **Locked/Recharge/Struggle request branches.** These descriptors
>   currently block opt-in decisions. Support them in the decision
>   descriptor wherever the request data is exact (the legal action is the
>   single emitted option), so the decision callback isn't blocked.
>   Canonical legal actions remain the only action authority.
>
> **Proof** (born sessions):
> - exact emitted vs omitted values for each flag, for both owners;
> - a choice-locked turn and an Encore turn: request restriction known,
>   reason known only after public proof;
> - a Recharge turn and a Struggle turn: the descriptor supports the

> - an Arena Trap or Shadow Tag `trapped` emission vs a suppressed case
>   (unknown, not false);
> - `maybeDisabled` stays actor-level, and the per-move cells stay blocked;
> - default output stays byte-identical; copied, foreign or stale handles
