> ### Part B — coverage registry
>
> 1. **Source of truth for rows:**
>    `docs/contracts/fixtures/perspective-knowledge-v1/property-contexts.json`
>    (61 properties, about 81 applications of `{context, scope, subject,
>    coverage}`). Create one registry row per application; scope `both` becomes
>    two rows, `perspective: "own"` and `perspective: "opponent"`. Generate the
>    row skeleton with a small script (`tools/registry/generate_skeleton.py`)
>    so it can't drift from the contract.
> 2. **File:** `docs/contracts/knowledge-coverage.json`, with:
>    - `schema: "knowledge-coverage/v1"`;
>    - `rows[]`, each with `property`, `family` (K01–K20, from `profiles.json`
>      obligations), `context`, `perspective` (`own`/`opponent`), `status`
>      (`adopted` | `blocked` | `legitimately_unknown` | `missing`),
>      `provider` (file:function or null), `tests` (an array of
>      `"<test file>::<exact test name>"`), `limits` (short text),
>      `blocked_by` (text or null) and `impact` (`high` | `medium` | `low`).
> 3. **Initial content** comes from the reconciliation table in
>    `docs/planning/perspective-fidelity-prompt58-reconciliation.md`, adjusted
>    for Prompt59 (original loadout/details/stats) and Prompt60 (current
>    request-declared stats/details/cosmetics). Use their reports and lead
>    reviews in `docs/planning/`. Map the dispositions this way:
>    - **A** → `adopted`, with the limits written in `limits` (A is always
>      scoped, so don't widen it);
>    - **U**, **MISSING** and **P** → `missing`;
>    - **L** → `legitimately_unknown`, for that perspective only;
>    - **DECIDE** → `blocked`, with the decision named;
>    - the Prompt59 blockers (partial EV/IV tuples, random-gender-dependent
>      details) → `blocked`.
>
>    When in doubt, choose `missing`. Over-claiming is the one error this
>    registry exists to prevent.
> 4. **Impact** (initial; Claude will review): `high` means it affects most
>    decisions (HP/status, boosts, hazards and side conditions, weather and
>    terrain, items/abilities current and opponent, Tera, current moves/PP,
>    trapped/lock/choice, active slot, form/types). `low` covers cosmetics
>    (ball, shiny), original-only metadata (happiness, submitted EV/IV) and
>    rare incarnation lineage. Everything else is `medium`.
> 5. **Test** `sim-core/tests/knowledge_coverage.test.ts` (it's in `test:fast`)
>    checks that:
>    - the rows equal the generated skeleton exactly (no missing, extra or
>      duplicate rows);
>    - every `adopted` row has at least two `tests` entries, and every listed
>      test name exists verbatim in its file **and that file is in the
>      `test:fast` Node or Python list** (so adopted claims are verified on
>      every run);
>    - every `adopted` row cites at least one negative test (its name or
>      `limits` makes clear that it covers unknown, omitted, foreign or
>      rejection);
>    - `legitimately_unknown` is allowed only where the family's profile
>      `unknown`/`opponent_unknown` lists permit it for that perspective, and
>      the row's `limits` names the reason (`hidden_opponent`, `hidden_timer`
>      or `not_emitted`);
>    - `blocked` rows have a non-empty `blocked_by`.
>
>    It also prints a summary: counts by status, and `missing` by impact.
