> **Appendix — prototype logic (scratch tool used for the probe)**
> - Input: replay JSON from `replay.pokemonshowdown.com/<id>.json`. Feed
>   `inputlog` lines to `BattleStream`, dropping `>version` and `>chat`.
> - Spectator view of the simulator output: for each `|split|pN` block, the
>   next two lines are the secret and public renderings. A public-log line
>   matches if it equals either one (public logs can show exact HP).
> - Ignore these line types on both sides: `t:`, `j`/`J`, `l`/`L`, `n`/`N`,
>   `c`/`c:`, `raw`, `html`, `uhtml`, `uhtmlchange`, `inactive`, `inactiveoff`,
>   `player`, `gametype`, `gen`, `tier`, `rated`, `rule`, `teampreview`,
>   `seed`, `timer`, `debug`, `request`, `error`, `sideupdate`, `badge`, and
>   empty lines.
> - Forfeits and inactivity losses aren't in the input log. Truncate the public
>   log at `|-message|… forfeited.` or `… lost due to inactivity.`, and the
>   simulator output to the same length.
> - Report per replay: id, version, rating, ending, matched prefix length,
>   full match, divergence turn, and the first differing sim/public lines.
