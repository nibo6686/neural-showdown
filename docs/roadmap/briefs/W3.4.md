>   from public chronology, starting from the appearance's details.

>   - `detailschange` is permanent and survives switching.
>   - `-formechange` is temporary and resets on switch-out or faint.
>   - Cover the Gen 9 randbats cases: Palafin Hero, Mimikyu Busted, Eiscue,
>     Minior, Cramorant, Morpeko, Ogerpon and Terapagos Tera forms, and
>     Shaymin's frozen reversion, wherever the engine emits each one.
> - **Types.** Current types come from species and form, then public type
>   changes, applied in order:
>   - `-start|typechange` (Soak, Reflect Type, Burn Up, Double Shock, Conversion);
>   - `typeadd` (Forest's Curse, Trick-or-Treat);
>   - Roost removing Flying for the turn.
>
>   Every one resets on switch-out. Tera overrides defensive types, except
>   Stellar, which keeps the original typing.
> - **Tera.**
>   - **Own:** the Tera type is known from the request. Whether the Pokémon
>     has terastallized comes from the request and the public
>     `-terastallize`.
>   - **Opponent:** unknown until `-terastallize|…|Type`. From then on it's
>     known and **persists across switches** for the rest of the battle.
>     Never infer it from defensive types (Stellar).
> - **Transform.**
>   - `-transform` (Transform, Imposter) is public. The copied form, types
>     and boosts are public. Copied moves are W7 territory and stay
>     `blocked`.

>   - **Identity never changes** (W3.3).
