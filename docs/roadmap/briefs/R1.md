> ### Requirements
> 1. **Resilient writes.** Journal and manifest writes retry on
>    `EBUSY`/`EPERM`/`EACCES` with backoff (up to about 60 s), opening files
>    with shared modes. A single failed write must never end the run. If
>    writes stay blocked past the backoff, pause, record a `blocked-write`
>    status in `*.run.json`, and keep retrying.
> 2. **Supervisor with auto-resume.** Add `tools/replay_repro/supervise.cjs`.
>    It launches the census as a child and, on any unexpected exit
>    (crash, kill, OOM), **restarts it with resume** after a backoff (cap
>    about 20 restarts, then stop with a clear status). It writes a heartbeat
>    and status (`running`/`restarting`/`complete`/`gave-up`) to `*.run.json`
>    at least every minute. A normal completion writes the final summary and
>    exits 0. Resume stays keyed by replay ID, is idempotent, and requires the
>    same commit and corpus manifest hash.
> 3. **Keep the machine awake while running, without changing settings.**
>    While the supervisor is alive, hold
>    `SetThreadExecutionState(ES_CONTINUOUS | ES_SYSTEM_REQUIRED)`, for
>    example through a small PowerShell P/Invoke helper that holds it while
>    the supervisor PID lives and then exits. It must release on exit. **No
>    `powercfg`, registry or power-plan changes.** Document that a closed lid
>    or a manual Sleep still sleeps; requirements 2 and 4 handle that.
> 4. **Sleep and time-jump tolerance.** Per-replay timeouts use a monotonic
>    clock. Detect suspension, either a heartbeat gap well beyond the tick
>    interval or a wall-clock/monotonic jump. On detection, treat in-flight
>    replays as **interrupted** and re-run them; **never** record a timeout
>    or failure outcome for a replay that was suspended. A genuine timeout
>    outcome is final only after one retry.
> 5. **Concurrency hygiene.** Documented readers (watchdogs, status checks)
>    must use the status file or open the journal with read/write sharing.
>    Add a note to `tools/engine/README.md`.
>
