> **Slice P0.5a — simulation-only environments, and moving the raw-snapshot mechanics tests onto them.**
> Repo: `C:\Users\cloud\Downloads\neural\final`, branch `main-native11`
> (after `d3c2c82`). Read `AGENTS.md` (no delegation, no full suites) and
> `docs/roadmap/LONG-TERM-PLAN.md` §1 first.
>
> **Problem.** About 423 baseline-red tests across about 40 files build a
> `Battle` by hand (`tests/helpers/state_lifecycle.ts`, inline `new Battle`,
> `Teams.import`), then call `env.resetFromSerialized(battle.toJSON())`
> without a ticket, or patch `LocalBattleEnv.prototype.resetWithOptions` to do
> so. Since P38, the pinned engine's policy is `held-b2-required`
> (`owner_identity_crosswalk.ts:snapshotRestorePolicy`), and
> `assertRestoreAdmission` rejects any restore without a ticket issued from a
> committed parent boundary. That is correct for the authoritative pipeline:
> a raw snapshot can't prove actor identity or owner knowledge. But two things
> legitimately need to simulate arbitrary states without that authority:
>
> - **mechanics tests**, which check observations, requests and legality for a
>   constructed state;
> - **future search and rollouts** (plan Phase 2 B4), which clone a state with
>   sampled hidden information and simulate ahead. Belief forks are blocked
>   under native11 for the same reason (`assertBeliefForkPolicy`).
>
> **Decision (Claude, as planner).** Add an explicit **simulation-only**
> environment mode that may raw-restore and has **no authority**, instead of
> loosening the authoritative policy or rewriting hundreds of tests to reach
> their states by play.
>
> ### Part A — the mode (in `src`)
> 1. Add an explicit way to create one, such as
>    `LocalBattleEnv.createSimulationOnly(options)` or an equivalent factory.
>    The authoritative constructors and `PipelineIntegrationSession.create`
>    must be unable to produce one, and a simulation-only env must be unable to
>    become authoritative.
> 2. In simulation-only mode:
>    - `resetFromSerialized` accepts raw snapshots with no ticket. The existing
>      snapshot/terminal-history/override validation still runs.
>    - No `NativeObjectAssociation`, native birth, original-owner creation or
>      request-health receivers are attached.
>    - Every authority surface throws `simulation-only/v1/no-authority`: owner
>      knowledge and projections, owned decision input, native object
>      holdings, restore tickets, `captureSeededSnapshot` used as a
>      held-restore parent, and use inside `PipelineIntegrationSession`.
>    - Step results carry a top-level marker, for example
>      `authority: "simulation-only"`, and `validatePipelineEpisodeRecordBundle`
>      and the Python `pipeline_record` reader reject anything containing it.
>    - Observations, requests, legal actions and canonical actions work as
>      normal. They're still perspective-filtered: simulation-only means
>      "no identity authority", not "no privacy".
> 3. The default authoritative behavior is unchanged. `held-b2-required` stays.
>    Existing admission negatives (`snapshot_admission`,
>    `raw-restore-unsupported`, `foreign-or-stale-restore-ticket`) must still
>    pass.
> 4. Don't unblock belief forks in this slice. Note in the report how the mode
>    would support them later.
>
> **Test** (new, `sim-core/tests/simulation_only.test.ts`; add it to
> `test-fast-files.json`), covering:
> - a raw `toJSON()` restore works and produces correct p1/p2 requests and
>   observations;
> - each authority surface throws;
> - a session can't wrap the env, and a bundle containing a simulation-only
>   result is rejected in TS and by `python -m neural.pipeline_record` (exit 2,
>   empty stdout);
> - an authoritative env still rejects the same raw snapshot;
> - simulation-only observations don't leak the other side's private request.
>
