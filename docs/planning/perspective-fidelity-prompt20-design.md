# Prompt20 proposed persistence boundary

Design only, 2026-10-11. Frozen PERSPECTIVE_KNOWLEDGE semantics remain the basis;
its historical status text does not reopen accepted Prompt10 decisions. No wire,
profile, writer, identity or source authority is adopted here.

## Minimal rule and representation

Persist **what an appearance executed**, even when its immutable roster carrier is
unknown. Never restore a move list merely because two displays match. Distinguish:

| Layer | Subject and proof | Lifetime / inference limit |
|---|---|---|
| Roster knowledge | Immutable original-delivery identity, or independently proved causal roster crosswalk | Historical proved facts persist; current freshness is separate |
| Appearance | Side + monotone switch/drag stint number, exact source start/end indices | Position/name/species/level/extractor slot are display attributes |
| Execution | Appearance subject, exact record/index and exclusive observed cursor, move token, execution-origin evidence | Append-only, including duplicates at different indices; direct execution is not possession |
| Current executable moves | Fresh owner-addressed active move inventory, with move incarnations and emitted PP/flags | Ends at new boundary/wait/switch/terminal unless newly proved; no replay requests |
| Starting originals | Separate approved owner provider or owner-only full-source original-possession certificate | Immutable, never overwritten by Transform, Mimic, Sketch or called moves |

Propose an **internal** appearance/execution ledger, reconstructible solely from an
admitted selected prefix. Appearance keys use the frozen `p1|p2:appearance:N`
subjects; roster keys use immutable original slots, never mutable party positions.
An execution receipt needs appearance, source index, observed_at=index+1, exact
record/move and origin evidence. A separate optional crosswalk carries carrier,
basis, causal availability and witnesses. No new content ID algorithm. Source trace
uses Prompt19's original indices; routed indices remain separate. Future wire
representation must be explicitly reviewed against frozen subject/fact schemas.

Frozen `move_execution` is a single text value on `roster_or_appearance`; it is not
a receipt-array schema. At most one fact per logical key in a context prohibits
encoding all executions as duplicate facts or overloading effect_key. A selected
latest execution can use that property with its evidence, while older receipt
chronology remains in raw prefix/audit parent history or a separately reviewed
internal result. No compound value or new consumer history property is adopted.

Every receipt initially remains with its source appearance. A proved association
can add a carrier relation without overwriting old evidence or treating every move
as current/original. A supported nonunique mapping retains ambiguity. A missing or
unimplemented carrier/writer provider is **blocking/unsupported**, not a convenient
`ambiguous_carrier` success verdict. Display ambiguity can be recorded diagnostically
without discharging frozen assurance obligations.

## Boundary operations

Switch/drag closes the outgoing stint, opens a fresh appearance and clears temporary
execution/request authority. Historical receipts and proved roster knowledge survive.
No prior display row is copied wholesale into a new appearance. Current-stint events
without a switch/replace do not create another carrier; their earlier execution
receipts persist by chronological continuity, even while roster identity is unknown.

Illusion `replace` updates disclosure for the existing stint. Pre-reveal executions
remain attached to that stint, not the impersonated teammate. A causal disclosure
may refine the carrier only with a supported unique mapping. Restoration of the
impersonated teammate opens a separate appearance, preserving that teammate's
earlier receipts and the imposter's separately. No first nickname match or union of
both move arrays is an identity proof.

Transform records temporary state, not starting membership. Pinned
`pokemon.ts:1251–1272` installs virtual current slots; `1449–1459` restores base slots
on reset. Mimic changes temporary moveSlots (`moves.ts:12280–12293`); Sketch changes
both current/base slots (`17198–17202`), making acquisition persist through reset
without making it a starting original. Called execution is retained with its call
evidence; bare execution cannot prove how a move was obtained. Unsupported or
unclassified relevant origins block attribution instead of being labelled direct
possession. PP, maxPP, disabled, target availability and legal actions require
their own legitimate delivery, not inference from execution.

## Request and original binding

Only the addressed owner's fresh request, joined to independently retained delivery
and immutable carrier/crosswalk evidence, can refine private current knowledge.
Request active rows prove current addressed data, but `side.pokemon` indices swap:
`battle-actions.ts:127–130`. Duplicate nicknames defeat a first-match join. Require
origin, owner, exact causal prefix, delivery sequence/cursor, unique active mapping
and independent roster association; conflicting mappings reject. Until providers
exist, retain public appearance receipts without manufacturing private bindings.
Foreign owner, wrong origin/prefix, future delivery and stale actionable request
must not enter the perspective. Adversarial misdelivery currently reaches legacy
extractor self_team; this is a separate request-source/binder dependency, not a
Prompt20 repair. Accepted replay adapter rejects all requests for both owners.

Owner replay starting policy may use a separately proved full-source certificate
and starting-only crosswalk retrospectively. It cannot relabel earlier chronological
public appearances, public proofs or current IDs; opponent future revelations stay
excluded. A later request without continuity proof cannot retroactively relabel
earlier execution. Hand-authored model bindings/certificates illustrate policy only,
not trusted registries or complete runtime certificates.

## Real fixture decisions

Random U-turn53 and128 survives in the raw trace, but fresh displays at106/243 lose
structured revelation. Random has no roster preview; repeated display, format or
future absence of Illusion revelation is insufficient to merge carriers. OU Ice
Beam45 loses revelation at119. OU preview18–23 and Species Clause11 offer a possible
future public certificate route; they do not currently supply a reviewed crosswalk.
Pinned public preview uses actual details (`rulesets.ts:632–640`), while switch
displays can use Illusion (`pokemon.ts:510–530`). Species Clause emission alone is
not an attestation that team validation or all relevant continuity writers ran.
OU remains diagnostic/out-of-profile. Identity is unproved **in the accepted path**,
not declared unknowable forever.

The frozen fixture ledger's true revelations on the new appearances express the
strong retention goal; its unverified source-authority note supplies no identity
certificate. Preserve that ledger and its red evidence. This checkpoint requires
retaining historic executions, while source-proved cross-switch active ownership is
a separate gate. Future publication must meet the stronger knowledge goal, not just
this evidence-relative subset.

## Flat BattleView and smallest next scope

Flat `BattleView` cannot represent both old appearance knowledge and a new ambiguous
active display accurately. It lacks appearance IDs, execution origins, provenance,
carrier uncertainty and move incarnation/freshness; `moves`/`revealed_moves` conflate
categories. The admitted raw prefix still retains evidence, but structured lists
lose it on switch. Blind list union can pass the old fixture assertions while
contaminating Illusion/duplicate cases. No reinterpretation of these arrays is
authorized by this proposal.

Proposed first production write set for lead approval: **new**
`sim-core/src/public_move_history.ts`, **new** `sim-core/tests/public_move_history.test.ts`
and bounded evidence. Start with a pure internal appearance/execution ledger over
accepted prefixes, unbound carrier relations and classified/unsupported origins;
reuse Prompt20 literal oracles. Prove both-owner real receipts before/after switches,
duplicates, Illusion, no suffix reads, deterministic restore/rebuild and zero invented
originals/PP/actions. An internal unit alone is not repair: the same approved boundary
must authorize explicit wiring, minimally `sim-core/src/replay_import.ts` plus its
test, returning a **separate internal history result** while preserving existing
trace/view/IDs. Lead must choose that return shape before implementation. No existing
BattleView/schema/caller change is implied.

Cross-switch roster projection then needs a separately reviewed source-provider/
causal crosswalk boundary (request/original delivery, or public certificate writer).
Consumer-visible representation and any schema/provider adoption need their own
explicit authorization and independent privacy/source/identity review. Starting
certificate implementation, passive ability/item repairs, move legality and production
request transport remain outside this next minimal receipt-retention scope.
