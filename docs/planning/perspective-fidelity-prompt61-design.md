## Emitted flags and the real scope mismatch

Capture presence and literal values from the same canonical `getMoveRequestData`/getMoves return and selected receipt. Per-option `disabled` is a **request-declared** Boolean; false under restricted data is not proof that no hidden disabling exists. Lock/Recharge omit it. `trapped`/`maybeTrapped` are roster-actor facts when emitted true; omission remains unknown, never false (the writer can suppress both when no switch-in or under other branches). Do not publish native `trapped`/volatiles directly.

`maybeDisabled` and `maybeLocked` are **actor-level aggregate flags**. `getMoveRequestData` may set/clear them while restricting information. Emit them as `owned_moves.request.emitted_flags`, with actor subject, presence, literal true and affected_options=unspecified. Their omission is unknown. Do not broadcast maybeDisabled=true as a known flag on every move. The frozen K08 `maybe_disabled` application is per move: those cells remain blocking unsupported_writer until a reviewed attribution rule or separately approved representation exists. `maybeLocked` is uncertainty, not the K08 definite `lock` fact. No named lock/cause is authorized by a single option, trapped flag or private getLockedMove return. Definite lock adoption needs a separately qualified writer/public rule. These aggregate flags can share this batch as informational metadata; canonical candidates alone still authorize actions.
