# Prompt47 — bounded native original-owner source adoption design

Prepare one implementable B4 design for the project lead using the retained 0.11.11 candidate. Read Prompt46 acceptance, existing perspective/request-knowledge contracts, `perspective_knowledge_source`/original-owner provider APIs, and the accepted B1/B2/B3/delivery seams. No production changes or validation runs.

## Objective and first scope

Connect authentic retained original owner roster/loadout to the existing starting/current knowledge representation for each owner. Propose the smallest useful native-only consumer boundary: starting roster and original species, ability, item and move facts, with unknown/known_absent only where the legitimate source proves them. Do not bundle all knowledge families or complete-context claims.

Starting originals stay immutable across copy/acquisition/form/Transform effects. Current facts and legal actions remain separately governed by public chronology/current authority and the owned request. Accepted direct-switch history may link its exact supported appearance/event to an original slot; it is not a generic resolver for other actors or hidden opponent facts.

## Required design

- Map trusted private creation/source retention and current/restored associations to a version-bound native11 source entry. Show exact owner/runtime/session/world/parent authority and failure/close handling. Public holdings, source IDs, equal bytes, profiles, copied tokens and primitive switch metadata cannot mint source authority.
- Inspect existing historical origin quarantine and catalog/checker contracts. Propose an additive target-native admission path that preserves historical readers/attestations and replay exclusions. Separate raw evidence, source-authorized facts, completeness and capability claims. Any necessary schema/catalog/compatibility decision must be explicit for lead approval; no silent relabeling.
- Identify actual producer→knowledge-builder→consumer callsites and how both owners obtain starting facts at the original boundary and held continuation without opponent disclosure. Decide whether a first useful boundary can remain TS-private and which Python/publication adoption is a necessary dependency versus later work. Do not design a general broker/certification platform.
- Define original move possession vs current/copied/called moves, original/base vs current ability, absent vs consumed/transferred item, and unbound appearance behavior. Never backfill opponents or derive missing originals from species defaults, generator priors or later acquired evidence.
- Specify immutable retained starting lineage, current cutoffs, knownness/precision/lifecycle and owner isolation within the finite scope. Incomplete/unimplemented families remain blockers for aggregate strong completeness rather than allowed unknown success.
- Propose a small future acceptance matrix grounded in real provider/caller integration: both owners, initial effects, held restore/reorder, private/foreign/copied source attacks, suffix/history immutability and no output contamination. Reuse accepted B1/B2/B3 algorithms; do not repeat their full tests or demand missing broad mechanics as prerequisites unless a concrete dependency requires them.
