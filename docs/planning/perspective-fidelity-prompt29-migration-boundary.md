## Snapshot, record and replay policy requiring lead adoption

- Existing native snapshots encode unversioned engine internals; their schema and fingerprint are not proof of producer version. `State` reference/order methods are identical, but generic enumeration serializes new/renamed native fields. `regressionForme`→`formeRegression`, `ppUps`, `maybeLocked`, `bondTriggered` and `heroMessageDisplayed` make cross-version restoration unsafe to infer.
- Keep old snapshot bytes/hashes and their 0.11.10 runtime together. The 0.11.11 lane admits only its own held same-runtime session snapshots, or externally catalogued same-version legacy snapshots with verified provenance. Unknown/cross-version imports are unsupported; a syntactically successful fromJSON is not approval. Because the wire snapshot lacks an engine field, enforce version association at the owning artifact/session boundary or obtain a separately reviewed schema decision. Do not infer it from labels/sets/hashes or change old envelopes in place.
- New 0.11.11 records receive their honest simulator revision. Native state/log/request changes can change fingerprints, branch/transition IDs and outputs. Keep canonical algorithm vectors and old literal records unchanged; add distinct target vectors. Cross-version equal-seed/equal-ID/equal-output assertions are not gates. A same-version uninterrupted/restore comparison is.
- Preserve raw replay lines and source indices, including `[silent]`. Offer explicit exact-form compatibility profiles: historical 0.11.10 Natural Cure form; target 0.11.11 ordered `[from] ability: Natural Cure`, `[silent]` form; optional audited replay union of these two forms. A replay's date/form is not an engine revision certificate. Unknown engine provenance can receive bounded grammar admission, never native source authority, B1/B2 mappings or completeness. Keep target/native and replay compatibility claims separate. Unknown/misordered/duplicate tags and unrelated abilities with `[silent]` reject.
- Keep reported five-replay results scoped as reported diagnostics. The saved-input inventory does not prove exact emitter versions or complete terminal admission; do not normalize the blocked replay or rewrite prior results.
