## Goal

Both players receive all required information legitimately available to their perspective throughout the supported battle. Original owner knowledge comes from approved addressed evidence; current/public state stays chronological. Public replay reconstruction separately backfills uniquely attributable own starting facts revealed anywhere from turn 1, because a live owner already knows their starting team. Never-revealed and ambiguous replay facts stay unknown. This is stronger than complete capture, not omniscient access to hidden opponent/RNG state.
