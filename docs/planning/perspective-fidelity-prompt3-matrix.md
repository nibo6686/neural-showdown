## Replay policy and actual integration audit

The requested policy is **full public replay → perspective owner's uniquely attributable STARTING facts at turn 1**. Never-revealed or ambiguously attributed facts stay unknown. Opponent and dynamic state remain at the exact prefix. Later public evidence may reveal an original consumed item, but only if its original ownership is established and no earlier transfer makes attribution ambiguous; consumption time is never backdated. Acquired/copied moves/items/abilities and transformed attributes are never initial loadout. The fact's source event can be later than the observation only in this explicit owner-starting context. It must not become `direct_observed` causal belief evidence or a private request.

## Boundary coverage requirements

For every K/R family, use two owners and independently expected truth at **each** admitted boundary, including intermediate nonactor evidence. Test original request, first pre-decision, post-resolution, ordinary switch/drag/re-entry, consumed/waiting request, one-sided force switch, Revival selection and subsequent wait, no-request terminal, restored nonterminal continuation and restored terminal. Accepted barriers must not expose a transient pre-settled requestless output. A restored terminal-only result may expose supported state but does not establish original-starting coverage without a validated predecessor.

Positive expectations come from **independently captured addressed requests and exact public records**, with pinned writer semantics for transformations and lifecycle. Reading private engine state is useful only as a source-mechanics witness and exclusion check; it is not the player knowledge oracle. Never use `projectPublicTypedState`, `projectPublicItems`, `projectObservableBattleState`, reconstructed-team helper or a matching TS/Python port to manufacture expected truth. Separate golden tables/fact ledgers should be hand-audited against source and raw records. Cross-language agreement alone can reproduce the same omission.

For causal simulator/live observations, append arbitrary future suffixes and prove earlier snapshots/identities/knownness invariant. For replay, only owner-attributed starting facts may change with a later revealing suffix; opponent/dynamic state/requests/actions at earlier prefixes remain byte-for-byte invariant. Mutate truth while resealing observation/belief/transition/envelope identities and joins; wrong-owner evidence, future event citations, lost known facts, invented zero/absence, mixed policy/source and unsupported true claims must reject before Python output.
